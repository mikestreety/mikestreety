Racing friends indoors usually means the strongest rider wins every week. IRL evens that out, so a group with very different fitness can race each other properly.

1. **Ride a qualifier** - everyone rides the same route once, flat out, which sets your climbing, sprinting and flat numbers
2. **Race when it suits you** - each race is a set route with a window, usually two weeks
3. **Beat your prediction** - whoever beats their predicted time by the most wins, even if they were last over the line

Your numbers move towards how you actually rode after each race, so getting fitter is still rewarded and anyone who took it easy in the qualifier soon gets found out. Bonus points for the fastest climbs and sprints give the quickest riders something to chase.

### How it's built

It's a static **11ty** site, scored at build time from the Strava leaderboards and rebuilt overnight so race windows stay up to date. League pages are private and only show initials, places and points - times are never published.
