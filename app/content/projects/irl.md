Close racing, whatever your level. Indoor Racing League scores each rider against their own predicted time, so a group of friends with very different fitness can race each other properly.

### How it works

1. **Ride a qualifier** - everyone rides the same route once, flat out. It gives you three numbers: how you compare with the middle rider on climbs, sprints and the flat.
2. **Race when it suits you** - each race is a set route with a window, usually two weeks. A flat route leans on your flat number, a climbing route on your climb number.
3. **Beat your prediction** - your time is compared with what you were expected to ride. Whoever beats their prediction by the most wins, even if they were last over the line.

After each race your numbers move part of the way towards how you actually rode, so getting fitter is still rewarded and anyone who took it easy in the qualifier soon gets found out. Bonus points go to the fastest riders on the climbs and sprints, so the quickest riders still have something to chase.

### How it's built

It's a static **11ty** site, scored at build time from the Strava leaderboards and rebuilt overnight so race windows stay up to date. League pages are private and only show initials, places and points - times are never published.
