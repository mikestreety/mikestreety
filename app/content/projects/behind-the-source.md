It started in 2020 with [interviews with 8 web folk](https://behindthesource.mikestreety.co.uk/interviews/) about how they work and how they got there.

In 2022 it became a [podcast](https://behindthesource.mikestreety.co.uk/podcasts/) for people who want to get into a specific topic, covering Svelte, Umbraco, jQuery, Design Systems, Gitpod and Coolify.

Listen on [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/behind-the-source/id1645278976), [subscribe with RSS](https://feeds.acast.com/public/shows/62d9b3f5f39c44001194bc1e), or find the episodes in the [talks section](/talks/).
