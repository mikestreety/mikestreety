Hovélo has no membership fees, no committee meetings, no racing and no-one in charge - the aim is to turn up on a Sunday and ride through the Sussex countryside. Ages and abilities vary, and it's about enjoying a ride in a group rather than tearing anyone's legs off.

Rides leave at 8am every Sunday from the Small Batch Coffee Company on Goldstone Villas, at an average of around 15-17mph. Routes are posted on the [Strava club](https://www.strava.com/clubs/94708) beforehand.

The website is a single hand-written HTML page on **Netlify**, with the [code on GitHub](https://github.com/hovelo/website). Read about [how it started](/blog/hovelo-a-new-hove-based-cycling-group/).
