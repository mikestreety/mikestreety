Hovélo is a group of friendly riders out to explore the Sussex countryside and to have fun.

Unlike other cycling clubs, there are no membership fees, no committee meetings, no racing and no-one in charge - the aim is to turn up on a Sunday and ride. Ages and abilities vary massively, and it's about getting used to riding in a group rather than tearing anyone's legs off (although there are some climbs). Rides average around 15-17mph.

Rides start at 8am every Sunday from the Goldstone Villas Small Batch Coffee Company, usually with a pre-ride coffee and a chat. Routes are posted on the [Strava club](https://www.strava.com/clubs/94708) beforehand, so you can see what you're getting into.

The website is a simple, hand-written HTML page hosted on **Netlify**, with the [code on GitHub](https://github.com/hovelo/website).
