## What is Cycle to Work?

Cycle to Work is a UK government scheme that lets employees get a bike (and accessories) through their employer, paying for it out of their salary before tax.

It works as salary sacrifice. The employer buys the bike, the employee hires it and their gross salary is reduced each month to cover the cost. Because the payments come out before income tax and National Insurance, the employee pays less than they would buying it outright. The employer saves a little on their National Insurance too.

## Things to know before you start

- **Minimum wage.** Salary sacrifice can't take someone's pay below the National Minimum Wage, so check this for anyone on a lower salary.
- **It affects other things.** A lower gross salary can affect things calculated from it, like pension contributions and some benefits. Let staff know, so they can check their own situation.
- **Leaving.** Decide what happens if someone leaves part way through the hire. Usually the remaining balance is deducted from their final pay.

## Why bother?

For a small agency, it costs us very little and it's a genuinely useful benefit. Office days are part of how we work, and making the commute cheaper and healthier for those who can cycle in seems like an easy win.
